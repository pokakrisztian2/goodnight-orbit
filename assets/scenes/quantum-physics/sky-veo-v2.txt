Breathtaking view from orbit at night, looking across the curved edge of the Earth. The glowing atmosphere is the star: thin luminous layers of deep blue, violet and soft gold hugging the horizon like a halo. Huge ribbons of aurora, green at the bottom and fading into violet and magenta at their tall tops, rise high above the atmosphere and ripple slowly like silk curtains in the wind. Above them, the bright core of the Milky Way arches across a sky full of countless stars. Far below, soft clouds, tiny golden city lights, and a few distant silent lightning flashes glowing inside storm clouds. A faint warm dawn glow at one end of the horizon. Photoreal, like the most beautiful ISS astronaut photograph, rich colour, deep blacks, extremely detailed. Locked-off camera, no camera movement, only slow gentle motion of the aurora and the Earth drifting very slowly. Calm, dreamy, awe-inspiring. No text, no spacecraft parts, no lens flare, no cuts.
