# Research — Cosmo Explains (@cosmoexplainsyt), 2026-09-28

A sleep-science channel that grew very fast. We studied it to copy what works and do it better.
Data: all 175 videos, pulled with yt-dlp on 2026-09-28.

## The numbers

- Started **2 April 2026**. ~6 months old.
- **175 videos. 30,500 subs. ~2.56 million views.**
- **1 video every day.** Every video ~2h10m (one is 3h25m).
- Median video (the middle one): **~5,100 views.** Average: 14,600.
- **The top 3 videos = ~30% of all views.** Most videos are small. A few hit very big.
- ~40% of videos stay under 3,000 views. Posting every day is how they find the hits.
- Best month so far: September 2026 (median 6,500 per video).
- Also posted on Spotify as a podcast. The Spotify link is the first line of every description.

## What is on screen (checked frames from the top 3 videos)

- **One illustrated room per video**, matched to the topic (relativity = living room,
  machine learning = study with equations on the wall, antennas = radio desk).
- **A robot mascot** (boxy, grey, big blue eyes) sits in the room. It barely moves.
- **A big window** shows real-looking space footage (black hole, Milky Way, Jupiter, nebula).
- **The window view changes every 30–60 s.** About 6 clips, and the set repeats every ~5.4 minutes.
- **Captions**: pixel font, bottom centre, a few words at a time.
- Video bitrate is tiny (~270 kbps). The picture almost never changes.

**Thumbnails:** always black background + one glowing colour (gold, cyan, red, yellow) +
huge topic word in the middle + small "Explained Slowly" + 6–10 labelled icons around it (a menu of the video).

## The script (top video, Relativity)

- ~16,500 words for ~2h07m → ~130 words per minute.
- **Opening:** a surprising human fact. *"It actually starts with a question so simple that a teenager asked it."*
- Then permission: *"You don't need to be a physicist… we're going to take our time."*
- Then **history as a story**. Start centuries before the famous person (Galileo before Einstein).
- **Ending:** second person, slow. *"wherever you are right now, eyes closed, breathing slow… spacetime is curving gently around you."*

## Their weak spots (our chance)

- **The robot has no story.** It is a mascot, not a character. Nothing to come back for.
- **Same room energy every night.** Nothing changes as you fall asleep.
- **No Shorts at all.** No funnel from short videos.
- **They re-post the same topics** (Nervous System, Neural Networks, Immune System 2–3 times).
- **They skip many AI topics:** transformers, GPUs, how CPUs think, how ChatGPT "thinks", search engines, GPS, crypto.
- Like/view ratio is low (~1–2%). Viewers are asleep. Subscribers come slowly.

## The voice (measured 2026-09-28 from the first 60 s of the Relativity video)

| | Cosmo | Our Kokoro "ASMR" test | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch (middle) | **84 Hz** (range 73–118) | 113 Hz | Cosmo is very deep. Bass-baritone. |
| Speed | **130 words/min** | ~110 | Normal, relaxed talking. Not slow. |
| Talking time | 66% | 60% | Keeps talking, short breaks. |
| Pauses | 23 per minute, **0.7 s** average, max 1.5 s | 0.9 s, max 2 s | Short, natural pauses. |
| Brightness | **2,400 Hz**, 30% above 4 kHz | 1,400 Hz, 13% | Cosmo is **clear and crisp**, close mic, some air. Our ASMR filter made ours too dark. |

**Lesson:** Cosmo is not a whisper and not slow. It is a **deep, clear, close, relaxed talker** at normal speed.

**Voice prompt (for Gemini TTS / voice design):**
> A deep, warm, bass-baritone male voice, around 35 to 45 years old, American accent. Calm, friendly and curious, like a smart friend explaining something fascinating late at night. Speak at a relaxed conversational pace, about 130 words per minute: not slow and dragged out, just unhurried. Low, steady pitch with small, natural rises on questions and interesting moments; never dramatic, never excited. Short natural pauses between sentences, a little longer after a question. Close to the microphone, clear and crisp, with soft breath and a little air in the voice, but NOT whispering and NOT muffled. Intimate and reassuring, like someone talking quietly next to you so they do not wake anyone. No radio-announcer energy, no sales tone.
