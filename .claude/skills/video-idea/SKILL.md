# Video idea

Talk to the user in short simple sentences. The user is Hungarian.

Ideas live in `ideas/ideas-backlog.md`. Read `STRATEGY.md` first.

## What makes an idea good here

An idea must pass all five:

1. **Something is gone.** Loss, ruin, decay, a way of life that died.
2. **Dual-intent.** It looks a bit strange or spooky from outside → people click.
   Inside it is real history → people trust it. 60% eerie / 40% history.
3. **It is real and researchable.** A named place, a named family, real dates. We do not invent.
4. **It can fill 12+ minutes** without repeating itself. Enough documented detail.
5. **It sits near rich advertisers**: houses, land, money, insurance, travel. That is the RPM.

Bonus points:

- It has **big numbers** (rooms, dollars, acres, years) → scale paragraph + title + thumbnail.
- It has a **legend** attached that we can leave open.
- It stays in the **same world** as our other videos (compounding).

## Score any idea 0–10

| Test | Points |
|---|---|
| Something is gone | 2 |
| Dual-intent (spooky outside, history inside) | 2 |
| Real, named, researchable | 2 |
| Enough material for 12+ minutes | 2 |
| Near rich advertisers | 1 |
| Has big numbers | 1 |

**7 or more → write it.** Under 7 → park it.

## Titles

- 50–65 characters. Calm. Curious. No CAPS, no "!!!", no "Top 10".
- A verified number is the strongest opener: *"The House With 160 Rooms and No Plan"*.
- Say the loss, or ask the binary question. Never both.
- Good: *"They Sealed the Door in 1961"*, *"The Family That Ran Out of Money and Kept Building"*.
- Bad: *"You WON'T BELIEVE This Abandoned Mansion 😱"*.

## Where to look for ideas

- Old newspaper archives (Chronicling America, local library digital archives)
- Wikipedia "List of..." pages for mansions, ghost towns, closed railroads, lost buildings
- National Register of Historic Places listings
- County historical society pages
- Old auction and probate records

Always keep the link. It goes in the script's Sources and in the video description.

## Output shape

When you propose ideas, give a table:

| Idea | Score | Why it clicks (the eerie half) | Why it is real (the history half) | Big number |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then say which one you would write first, and why. One recommendation, not five.
