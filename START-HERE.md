## The idea in 8 lines

1. Old US viewers watch long videos to the end, on the TV.
2. We do not make "content". We make **background cinema** — it plays while they cook or rest.
3. That keeps people on YouTube longer. YouTube's favourite thing. So it pushes us for free.
4. Ads aimed at old people pay a lot. So we earn more per view.
5. AI makes the pictures and the voice, so one video costs hours, not days.
6. Everyone else shouts. We whisper. Quiet thumbnails stand out.
7. Every video looks a bit spooky (they click) and delivers real history (they trust us).
8. Feeling = loss and nostalgia. That is what keeps them to the end.
