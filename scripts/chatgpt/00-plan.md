# ChatGPT — chapter plan (video 4)

Title: **Every Confusing Thing About ChatGPT Explained Slowly (For Sleep)**
Voice: same as videos 1–3 (123 words/min). Target ~12,500 words ≈ 100 min.
Facts: `research/chatgpt-facts.md`. UNCERTAIN items: "reportedly", "the story goes", or leave out.
Angle: **inside the machine, step by step, from the moment you press Enter.** Video 2 told the history; this one opens the box. One calm line back to video 2 ("on the machine learning night…"), never retell it.
Through-line: **one sentence travels through the machine.** Otto types one question at the start ("Why is the sky dark at night?"); every chapter follows that same sentence one step further; the last chapter is the answer arriving, word by word.
Otto: the Library of Words room — letters drifting like fireflies, the typewriter, the glass brain on the wall, tea.
