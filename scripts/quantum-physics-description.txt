Fall asleep while Otto, an astronaut on the night shift, slowly explains the most confusing parts of quantum physics: energy in steps, light as particles, the double-slit experiment, the uncertainty principle, Schrödinger's cat, entanglement, tunnelling, and quantum computers.

From Max Planck's glowing furnaces in 1900 to Einstein and Bohr arguing over breakfast, from a young man with hay fever on a North Sea island to the quiet rule that keeps you from falling through the floor, and the quantum physics hiding in your phone, your lamp and your GPS.

You don't need to understand all of it. Feynman said nobody does. If you fall asleep halfway, that's the plan.
