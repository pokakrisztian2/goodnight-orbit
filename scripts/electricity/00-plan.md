# Electricity — chapter plan (video 5)

Title: **Every Confusing Thing About Electricity Explained Slowly (For Sleep)**
Voice: same Gemini voice (~130 words/min measured on video 4). Target ~14,000 words ≈ 105–110 min. Each chapter file ~1,000 words (01 a bit more, it holds the welcome).
Facts: `research/electricity-facts.md` ONLY. UNCERTAIN items: leave out, or "the story goes" / "he later said". Never use the Edison 10,000-ways quote, Topsy, Faraday newborn-baby line as fact.
Room tonight: Otto's **Lightning Workshop** — a little Tesla coil curling violet sparks inside a glass dome, a plasma ball, old Edison bulbs on twisted cloth cables floating at different heights, copper coils, a hand-crank generator, Leyden jars, a stack of metal discs (a Volta pile), a toy kite with a key on its string, a brass galvanometer, tea.
Window tonight: a big calm thunderstorm over the ocean on the night side, lightning glowing inside the clouds, now and then a red sprite above it, coastal cities glowing gold.
Through-line: **"The electrons in a wire move slower than a snail. So why does the lamp come on the instant I touch the switch?"** Otto clicks his reading lamp off and on in ch.1 and wonders. Small callbacks in most chapters ("my lamp switch…"). Answer pieces: ch.6 (slow drift), ch.10 (fields, Faraday's lines), ch.11 (Maxwell, light), full answer in ch.14 (field outside the wire, Feynman, "impossible to get into bed before the room goes dark").
