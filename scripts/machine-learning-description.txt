Fall asleep while Otto, an astronaut on the night shift, slowly explains the most confusing parts of machine learning: neural networks, gradient descent, backpropagation, deep learning, AlphaGo, transformers, and how ChatGPT guesses the next word.

From Alan Turing's question "Can machines think?" to a perceptron that turned its own knobs with little motors, from the long AI winters to AlexNet trained in a bedroom, and why nobody fully understands what's inside today's AI.

You don't need to understand all of it. If you fall asleep halfway, that's the plan.
