Fall asleep while Otto, an astronaut on the night shift, slowly explains the most confusing parts of Einstein's theory of relativity: time dilation, spacetime, gravity, the speed of light, E=mc², the twin paradox, and black holes.

From Galileo's windowless ship cabin to Einstein's "happiest thought", from the 1919 eclipse to the GPS in your phone, atomic clocks that tick faster a foot higher, gravitational waves, and the first pictures of black holes.

You don't need to understand all of it. If you fall asleep halfway, that's the plan.
