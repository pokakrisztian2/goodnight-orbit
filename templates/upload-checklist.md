# UPLOAD CHECKLIST

Do these in order. Do not skip the AI box.

## Before upload

- [ ] Video is **10 minutes or longer** (aim 12–20).
- [ ] Cold open starts at second 0. No greeting, no intro animation.
- [ ] Works with eyes closed — the voice carries the whole story.
- [ ] Voice is calm, slow, and the same volume all the way through.
- [ ] Music is quiet, under the voice (about -20 dB).
- [ ] No image repeats more than once.
- [ ] Watched the first 60 seconds as a stranger. Does it hold?
- [ ] Thumbnail passes `thumbnail-brief.md`.

## Video details

- [ ] **Title** — calm, 50–65 characters, no CAPS, no "!!!".
- [ ] **Description** — first 2 lines say what the story is. Then 3–4 sentences. Then **chapter timestamps**. Then sources.
- [ ] **Chapters: one every 90–120 seconds** (6–8 for a 12-minute video; 12–18 only for long-form), pasted as timestamps (`0:00 Cold open`, `1:42 The Architect Who Refused To Stop`, ...). The first one must be `0:00`. Each headline implies conflict, never a plain label.
- [ ] **Tags** — 8–12, plain words people type.
- [ ] **Thumbnail** uploaded.
- [ ] **Playlist** chosen.

## The important boxes

- [ ] **"Altered or synthetic content" → YES.** We use an AI voice and AI images. YouTube asks this. Tick it. Not ticking it can get the video labelled or removed.
- [ ] **"Made for kids" → NO.**
- [ ] **Ads: ON**, and turn on **mid-roll ads** (only possible because the video is over 8 minutes). Place breaks at quiet moments, not mid-sentence.
- [ ] **Language: English (United States).**
- [ ] **Category:** Education or People & Blogs.

## In the description, always include

```
This video uses AI-generated images and an AI narrator to re-create
scenes we have no photographs of. Historical facts are sourced below.

Sources:
- 
- 
```

Being honest here protects the channel. It costs nothing.

## Publish

- [ ] Schedule for a good time for US older viewers: **Sat/Sun, 9–11am US Eastern** (that is 15:00–17:00 in Hungary).
- [ ] After publishing, watch the first 2 minutes on a TV or big screen.

## After 14 days and after 30 days

Write the numbers into `published/<video-name>.md`:

- Views
- CTR %
- Average view duration (minutes and %)
- RPM $ (if monetized)
- One line: what worked, what did not.
