# THUMBNAIL BRIEF — anti-design

> Full law, real examples and the checker: the **`thumbnail` skill**. This page is the quick sheet.

The thumbnail is a **positioning move**. It says who we are before they press play.

Everyone else shouts. We whisper.

---

## Never do this (this is the loud style)

- ❌ Big shocked face
- ❌ Red arrows, red circles
- ❌ Emojis
- ❌ Neon colours, extreme contrast
- ❌ Huge fat text across the whole picture
- ❌ More than 4 words of text

## Always do this

- ✅ **One picture.** One old house, one empty room, one lonely field.
- ✅ **Muted colours.** Faded, warm, old-photo feeling. Sepia, dusty blue, grey-green.
- ✅ **Small clean text.** 2–4 words. Bottom-left or bottom-right. A simple serif font.
- ✅ **Empty space.** Let the picture breathe. Empty space reads as "expensive" and "calm".
- ✅ **A person only from behind or far away**, if at all. Never a face looking at the camera.
- ✅ Must still be readable **small on a phone** and **big on a TV**. Check both.

---

## Text ideas that fit the whisper

- "Gone by 1974"
- "Forty rooms. Nobody left."
- "They never came back"

Not: "YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT HAPPENED 😱"

---

## Checklist before upload

- [ ] 1280 × 720, under 2 MB
- [ ] No face shouting
- [ ] 4 words or fewer
- [ ] Muted colours
- [ ] Looks calm next to loud thumbnails (open YouTube and compare)
- [ ] The picture matches the real story in the video (no lying)
