## The bad news: 2 weeks is not possible

To earn ad money on YouTube you need, right now:

- **1,000 subscribers**, **and**
- **4,000 public watch hours** in the last 12 months

A brand new channel cannot do that in 2 weeks. Nobody can. Not with AI, not with money, not with ads.

**Buying an already-monetized channel?** It is against YouTube's rules to sell accounts, YouTube re-checks monetization when the owner changes, it costs thousands of dollars, and the channel can be shut down after you pay. We are not doing that.

There is also a smaller tier at **500 subscribers** — but that only unlocks fan funding (memberships, Super Thanks). **No ad money.** Not what you want.

## The math (so you know exactly what to do)

4,000 hours = **240,000 watch minutes**.

Our video is 15 minutes. If people watch half of it, that is **7.5 minutes per view**.

240,000 ÷ 7.5 = **32,000 views total**.

Over 150 days that is:

- **~215 views per day**
- **~1,600 watch minutes per day**

That is not crazy. One good video that finds the right people does 30,000 views by itself.

**But be honest with yourself:** it is not guaranteed. It needs videos that people actually finish, and a bit of luck. Most channels take 6–12 months. We are trying to do it in 5.

## The subscriber problem

Watch hours usually come **before** subscribers on a calm, lean-back channel. People watch on the TV. They do not click subscribe on a TV.

So we push subscribers on purpose:

- One calm line at the end of every video: *"There are more quiet stories like this on the channel."*
- A pinned comment on every video with a link to the best playlist.
- Channel page set up properly: banner, "about" text, one featured video.
- Playlists by theme, so one video leads to the next.

**Target: 1,000 subs by January 2027.** Watch this number every week.
